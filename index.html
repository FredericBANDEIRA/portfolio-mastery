Core Quantitative Portfolio Formulas

Foundational equations governing portfolio risk, optimal weights, and excess return generation

$$\mathbf{w}^* = \frac{\mathbf{\Sigma}^{-1}(\mathbf{\mu} - r_f\mathbf{1})}{\mathbf{1}^T\mathbf{\Sigma}^{-1}(\mathbf{\mu} - r_f\mathbf{1})}$$
Tangency Portfolio
Optimal asset weights that maximize the Sharpe ratio along the Capital Allocation Line.
$$\text{MCR}_i = \frac{\partial \sigma_p}{\partial w_i} = \frac{(\mathbf{\Sigma}\mathbf{w})_i}{\sigma_p}$$
Marginal Risk Contribution
Exact rate of change in overall portfolio standard deviation as weight in asset $i$ increases.
$$\mathbf{\Pi} = \lambda \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{w}_{\text{mkt}}$$
Implied Equilibrium Returns
Black-Litterman neutral anchor: returns market clears at given current cap weights.
$$\text{IR} = \text{IC} \times \sqrt{\text{BR}}$$
Fundamental Law
Information Ratio broken into forecasting skill (Information Coefficient) and strategy breadth.
